Land Use & Civic Work

Current Role

Planning Commission

City of Everett Planning Commission
Commissioner
Current Appointment

Serving as a Planning Commissioner for the City of Everett, reviewing land use applications, development proposals, variance requests, and long-range planning decisions. The commission makes recommendations to the City Council on matters affecting Everett's built environment and growth strategy.

Focus areas include infill development, transportation impact analysis, fiscal sustainability of growth, and the relationship between land use decisions and Everett's broader housing and infrastructure needs.

  • Land use applications and conditional use permits
  • Comprehensive plan amendments
  • Development standards and zoning code review
  • Transportation and infrastructure impact assessment

Prior Service

Lake Stevens City Council

Lake Stevens City Council
Councilmember
2012 – 2019 · Seven Years

Seven years on the Lake Stevens City Council during a period of significant growth in Snohomish County. Participated in budget deliberations, land use policy decisions, utility planning, and municipal governance for a rapidly expanding community.

Developed a practical understanding of how local government budget constraints, state mandates, and community expectations intersect — experience that directly informs my current work on the Everett Planning Commission and my columns in the Herald.
